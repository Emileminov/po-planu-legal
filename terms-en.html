The app and the subscription

The core features of the app are free. The “Po Planu Pro” subscription unlocks extras: unlimited Lumi assistant (with a fair daily cap to prevent abuse) and the Lumi theme, icon and widget. Without a subscription Lumi is available in a limited way — a few requests per day.

Payment and renewal

  • The subscription is available monthly and yearly. The price is shown in the app before purchase and depends on your App Store country.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period.
  • If a free trial is offered, any unused part of it is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
  • You can manage the subscription and turn off auto-renewal in your Apple ID settings: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its rules: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Lumi assistant

Lumi uses a neural network to split your phrase into tasks. It can make mistakes: misread a date, a section or the wording. That is why you always see the result before anything is added and can correct or cancel it. Do not rely on Lumi for urgent, medical, legal or other important matters.

Do not use Lumi to submit other people’s personal data, illegal or abusive content, or to try to get around the service’s limits. Access to the assistant may be restricted in case of abuse.
